This new course from a proposed restructuring is larger than the preferred
size of $preferred_ects ECTS.

<course>
$course
</course>

These are its topics.
<topics>
$topics
</topics>

Decompose it into smaller, consequential courses when this is sound, e.g.:
- parts 1, 2, 3, ...: a sequence where each part builds on the previous one;
- fundamentals vs advanced: an introductory course and a deeper one depending
  on it;
- sub-groups of closely related topics that can be taught separately.

Requirements:
- parts are the new courses; together their topic_keys contain each topic
  above exactly once.
- Each part has ects of at most $max_ects; prefer $preferred_ects ECTS, and keep
  the parts' total close to the course's ECTS. A part may hold at most as many
  topic_keys as its ECTS.
- Part keys are new lowercase snake_case keys starting with "${course_key}_".
- prerequisites connect parts only (prerequisite_course_key ->
  dependent_course_key) and must be acyclic; prerequisites with other courses
  are rewired automatically.
- When the topics cannot be split soundly, return an empty parts array and an
  empty prerequisites array to keep the course whole.
