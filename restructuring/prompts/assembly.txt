The source topics of all courses have been grouped into the modules below.
Propose a rational restructuring of all source courses as a hierarchy of new
courses built from these modules.

Factor shared prerequisite knowledge into general courses reused across degree
programmes where useful, and keep specialist modules in specialist courses.
Prefer new courses that let each source course be replaced by few new courses
with little unrelated content.

These are the source courses expressed as the modules covering their topics.

Requirements:
- proposed_courses assigns one or more module keys (in topic_keys) to each new
  course; every module is used by at least one new course.
- prerequisites uses prerequisite_course_key -> dependent_course_key semantics
  and must form an acyclic graph.
- Source courses are mapped to new courses automatically from the modules, so
  do not list mappings.
