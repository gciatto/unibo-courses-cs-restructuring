Group the following batch of source topics into reusable teaching modules.

A module is a cohesive unit of knowledge that could be taught once and shared
by several courses (about 1-3 CFU of material). Group topics that are taught
together and that belong to the same subject; keep unrelated topics apart even
if they appear in this batch together. Very broad source topics may form a
module of their own.
Keep introductory material apart from advanced material, and generic
concepts apart from language- or technology-specific ones, so that courses
can later take only the basics they need.
